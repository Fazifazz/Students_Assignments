Starters

Crispy fried paneer cubes tossed with black pepper, curry leaves, green chilies, garlic, and onions for a crunchy, spicy starter.

Malabar Chicken Skewers

Juicy grilled chicken pieces marinated with Malabar spices, yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, curry leaves, and coconut oil for a smoky Kerala-style flavor.

Spiced Corn & Cheese Balls

Golden-fried balls made with sweet corn, mozzarella cheese, mashed potato, green chilies, coriander, and breadcrumbs, served with a flavorful dip.

Main Course

Fragrant basmati rice layered with spiced chicken, fried onions, mint, coriander, ghee, and whole aromatic spices, cooked in traditional Malabar dum style.

Kerala Fish Curry with Appam

Soft paneer cubes cooked in a rich tomato-butter gravy with cream, cashews, ginger, garlic, and mild Indian spices, paired with warm naan.

Chocolate Brownie Sundae

Fudgy chocolate brownie topped with vanilla ice cream, chocolate sauce, roasted nuts, and cocoa, creating a rich hot-and-cold dessert.
