Plant care 101

A little love goes a long way

Watering

Most houseplants prefer a deep soak when the top inch of soil feels dry — and slightly less in winter.

Light

Match the plant to the light you have. Bright indirect is the sweet spot for most tropical species.

Humidity

Group plants together or set them on a pebble tray to mimic the humidity of their native habitat.

Feeding

A balanced liquid feed every 4 weeks in spring and summer keeps growth lush and vibrant.
