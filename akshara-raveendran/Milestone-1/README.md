**Blossom \& Bloom**



**Project Overview**

Blossom \& Bloom is a responsive flower bouquet website designed to showcase fresh floral arrangements for different occasions.



**Key Features**

* Elegant and responsive floral website design
* Hero section with call-to-action buttons
* About section highlighting the brand
* Product section with 12 bouquet styles
* Occasion-based categories
* Customer testimonials and ratings
* Contact and order section
* Responsive navigation and layout
* Hover effects and smooth scrolling
* Product pricing displayed in Indian Rupees



**Technologies Used**

* HTML5
* CSS3
* Google Fonts
* External flower images

**Main Sections**

* Home
* About
* Shop
* Occasions
* Reviews
* Contact

**Design Highlights**

Responsive design for desktop, tablet, and mobile
