Every piece passes through our atelier before it reaches the floor — pressed, fitted and recorded in the house ledger.

Est. 1978

The House Story

Élara Vitale opened a single tailoring room above a leather workshop on Via dei Tornabuoni in the autumn of 1978. She kept one rule pinned to the wall: nothing leaves the room unless it can be worn in twenty years.

Her daughter added the Paris boutique in 1996 and the made-to-measure programme in 2004. The tailoring room still exists — three floors up, six people, the same ledger.

We release fifty pieces a season. When they are gone, they are not remade.
